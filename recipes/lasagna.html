Lasagna Recipe


Description

Lasagna is a timeless Italian classic — layers of silky pasta sheets, rich meat ragù, velvety béchamel sauce, and melted cheese, baked to golden perfection. Every bite is a warm, hearty embrace of flavors that have been loved for generations.


Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500 grams ground beef
  • 400 milliliters tomato passata
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoons salt
  • 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
  • 50 grams butter
  • 50 grams all-purpose flour
  • 500 milliliters whole milk
  • 0.3 teaspoons nutmeg
  • 200 grams mozzarella, shredded
  • 80 grams Parmesan, grated

Steps to make Lasagna

  1. Make the meat sauce: Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Sauté 1 onion, finely chopped for 20 minutesuntil soft, then add 3 garlic cloves, minced and cook for 1 more minute. Add 500 grams ground beef and brown it, breaking it apart. Pour in 400 milliliters tomato passata, season with 1 teaspoons dried oregano, 1 teaspoons salt, and 0.5 teaspoons black pepper. Stir well and simmer.

  2. Make the béchamel sauce: In a saucepan, melt 50 grams butter over medium heat. Whisk in 50 grams all-purpose flour and cook for 10 minutes . Gradually pour in 500 milliliters whole milk, whisking constantly to avoid lumps. Stir until the sauce thickens. Season with 0.3 teaspoons nutmeg and a pinch of 1 teaspoons salt.

  3. Preheat oven: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). If using dried lasagna sheets, boil them in salted water for 15 minutes and drain. No-boil sheets can be used directly.

  4. Assemble the lasagna: In a greased baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce. Place 12 lasagna sheets on top, then spread a layer of béchamel, followed by meat sauce. Sprinkle some 200 grams mozzarella, shredded. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with béchamel on top.

  5. Add cheese topping & bake: Sprinkle the top with remaining 200 grams mozzarella, shredded and all of the 80 grams Parmesan, grated. Cover loosely with foil and bake for 50 minutes . Then remove the foil and bake uncovered until the top is golden and bubbling.

  6. Rest before serving: Remove from the oven and let the lasagna rest before slicing and serving. This helps the layers hold together beautifully.
