A Great Greek Pasta Salad Recipe


Description

A delicious and refreshing Greek Pasta Salad made with tender pasta, crunchy vegetables, olives, and creamy feta cheese tossed in a flavorful Mediterranean dressing. This easy-to-make salad is perfect for lunch, dinner, picnics, or as a healthy side dish.


Ingredients

  • 250g pasta (penne or fusilli)
  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • ½ cup black olives
  • 100g feta cheese, crumbled
  • Fresh parsley or lettuce

Steps to make Great Greek Pasta Salad

  1. Cook the Pasta: Boil the pasta in salted water until perfectly tender. Drain and rinse with cold water to cool it down.

  2. Prepare the Vegetables: Chop the cucumber, tomatoes, onion, and capsicum into bite-sized pieces.

  3. Make the Dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, salt, and black pepper.

  4. Mix Everything: Add the cooled pasta and vegetables to a large bowl. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss well.

  5. Garnish and Serve: Top with olives and crumbled feta cheese. Chill for 20 minutes before serving for the best flavor.
