Taco Casserole Recipe


Description

A crowd-pleasing Mexican-inspired bake packed with seasoned ground beef, black beans, corn, and melted cheese — all layered over crunchy tortilla chips. It's taco night meets comfort food, ready in under an hour and guaranteed to disappear fast.


Ingredients

  • 500 grams ground beef
  • 1 taco seasoning packet
  • 120 milliliters water
  • 400 grams black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 200 grams corn kernels (canned or frozen)
  • 300 milliliters tomato salsa
  • 200 grams tortilla chips
  • 200 grams shredded cheddar cheese
  • 100 grams shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 tablespoons sour cream (for topping)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • 1 jalapeño, sliced (optional)

Steps to make Taco Casserole

  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Lightly grease a 9x13 inch baking dish and set aside.

  2. Cook the beef: Heat 1 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 onion, diced and cook for 8 minutes until softened. Stir in 3 garlic cloves, minced and cook for 1 more minute. Add 500 grams ground beef and brown it fully, breaking it into crumbles. Drain excess fat.

  3. Season & simmer: Stir in 1 taco seasoning packet and 120 milliliters water into the beef mixture. Mix well and let it simmer until the liquid is mostly absorbed. Add 400 grams black beans, drained and rinsed, 200 grams corn kernels (canned or frozen), and 300 milliliters tomato salsa. Stir everything together and remove from heat.

  4. Layer the casserole: Spread 200 grams tortilla chips evenly across the bottom of the greased baking dish. Spoon the beef and bean mixture evenly over the chips. Sprinkle 200 grams shredded cheddar cheese and 100 grams shredded mozzarella cheese generously over the top.

  5. Bake: Cover the dish loosely with foil and bake for 30 minutes . Remove the foil and bake for another 10 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden.

  6. Garnish & serve: Remove from oven and let it rest for 5 minutes . Top with dollops of 4 tablespoons sour cream (for topping), a sprinkle of 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish), and 1 jalapeño, sliced (optional) if using. Slice and serve hot.
